# Support

**Ask the GOAT team a question, report a problem or suggest an idea** without leaving GOAT. Every request becomes a support ticket: you follow the replies in GOAT, and they are also sent to you by email.

## The Support menu

Click the <code>Support</code> icon in the upper bar, on the right. On a phone, the same entries are in the account menu.

- <code>Support tickets</code>: **opens the list of your tickets**. A count next to it shows how many tickets wait for your answer or have replies you haven't read yet.
- <code>Report a problem</code>: **opens the form for a new ticket**.
- <code>Documentation</code>: **opens this documentation** in a new tab.

Tickets that need your attention are listed at the top of the menu, so you can open them directly.

:::info
On installations without support tickets, <code>Report a problem</code> opens an email to the support team instead, and there is no ticket list.
:::

## Create a ticket

<div class="step">
   <div class="step-number">1</div>
   <div class="content">In the <code>Support</code> menu, click <code>Report a problem</code>. You can also click <code>New ticket</code> on the <code>Support</code> page.</div>
</div>

<div class="step">
   <div class="step-number">2</div>
   <div class="content">Under <code>What do you need help with?</code>, <b>choose a category</b>: <code>Bug</code>, <code>How-to</code>, <code>Data issue</code>, <code>Feature request</code>, <code>Account & billing</code> or <code>Other</code>.</div>
</div>

<div class="step">
   <div class="step-number">3</div>
   <div class="content">Enter a <code>Subject</code> and a <code>Description</code>. For a bug, describe what you did, what you expected to happen and what happened instead.</div>
</div>

<div class="step">
   <div class="step-number">4</div>
   <div class="content">Under <code>How much is this blocking you?</code>, choose <code>Just a question</code>, <code>Slowing me down</code> or <code>Blocking my work</code>. This helps the team decide what to look at first. Feature requests skip this question.</div>
</div>

<div class="step">
   <div class="step-number">5</div>
   <div class="content">
   Optionally, add more context:
      <ul>
         <li><code>Related project</code>: the project the ticket is about. Not asked for account and billing tickets.</li>
         <li><code>Attachments</code>: screenshots, exported layers or logs. Up to 10 files, 10 MB each and 50 MB in total.</li>
         <li><code>Keep colleagues in the loop</code>: people from your organization who can read and reply, and get updates by email too.</li>
         <li><code>Attach technical details</code>: the page you came from, your browser and screen size. This is ticked by default.</li>
      </ul>
   </div>
</div>

<div class="step">
   <div class="step-number">6</div>
   <div class="content">Click <code>Submit ticket</code>. The ticket opens, and <b>you get a confirmation by email</b>.</div>
</div>

<div style={{ display: 'flex', flexDirection: 'column', alignItems: 'center' }}>
  <img src={require('/img/support/support_new_ticket.webp').default} alt="The New support ticket form" style={{ maxHeight: "auto", maxWidth: "100%", objectFit: "cover"}}/>
</div>

:::tip
A ticket is visible to you, the colleagues you add and your organization admins.
:::

## Follow your tickets

<div style={{ display: 'flex', flexDirection: 'column', alignItems: 'center' }}>
  <img src={require('/img/support/support_tickets.webp').default} alt="The Support page before the first ticket" style={{ maxHeight: "auto", maxWidth: "100%", objectFit: "cover"}}/>
</div>

The <code>Support</code> page lists your tickets under <code>Open</code> and <code>Closed</code>. Search by subject or ticket number. Each ticket shows its status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <code>Submitted</code> | The ticket has arrived and waits for the team. |
| <code>In progress</code> | Someone from the GOAT team is working on it. |
| <code>Needs your reply</code> | The team has asked you something. |
| <code>Resolved</code> | The team has solved it. |
| <code>Closed</code> | The ticket is closed. |

Open a ticket to **read the conversation and reply**. You can attach files to a reply, and you can also answer from your email. On the right you see who handles the ticket, the <code>People on this ticket</code> and its details. If you may manage the people on the ticket, you can add colleagues there, and you can always remove yourself.

When your problem is solved, click <code>Mark as resolved</code>. If it comes back, click <code>Reopen</code> or simply reply. Once a ticket is resolved, you can rate how the team did.

:::info
**Organization admins see every ticket of their organization.** On the <code>Support</code> page they can switch between <code>My tickets</code> and <code>Organization</code>. Members only see tickets they opened or were added to.
:::
