# Share Map

In the final step of our tutorial, we'll show you how to share your completed analysis with others.

1. **Click on `Share`** in the upper right corner of the map view
2. **Click on the `Public` tab**
3. **Click on `Publish`** 

🎉 **Done!** Your map is now publicly available.

4. Now you can:
-  🔗 **Copy URL**: Share the direct link so others can open the map in their browser.
- 🖥️ **Copy iframe code**: Embed the map in websites or tools that support HTML and iframes.
